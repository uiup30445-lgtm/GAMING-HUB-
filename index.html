Rules & Regulations

Booyah Arena Clash Squad Championship

1. Device & Gameplay

Only mobile devices are allowed. Emulators (PC/Bluestacks) or external triggers/hacks will lead to instant permanent disqualification.

2. Squad & UIDs

All 4 players must enter correct Free Fire UIDs during registration. Incorrect UIDs may cause slot cancellation.

3. Entry Fee & UTR

Entry fee is ₹100 per squad. Make sure to enter the correct 12-digit UPI Transaction ID (UTR) for quick admin verification.

4. Room Credentials

Custom room ID and password will be shared in the WhatsApp group 10 minutes before the match start time.
